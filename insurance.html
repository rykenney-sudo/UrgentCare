Understanding your costs

Copays, deductibles & what you’ll owe.

Insurance terms can be confusing. Here’s a plain-language guide to the costs that show up on a typical visit — so there are no surprises when you check in.

Copay

A fixed amount you pay for a covered visit — for example, $30 — usually collected at check-in. Your plan sets the amount.

Deductible

What you pay out of pocket each year before your insurance starts sharing costs. Once you meet it, your plan covers more.

Coinsurance

After your deductible, the share of a bill you still pay — say 20% — while your insurance covers the rest.

Out-of-pocket maximum

The most you’ll pay in a year. Once you reach it, your plan covers 100% of covered services for the rest of the year.

Putting it together: on a typical visit you’ll usually owe your copay at check-in. If you haven’t met your deductible yet, you may be billed for part of the visit afterward; once it’s met, coinsurance applies until you reach your out-of-pocket maximum.

FAQs

Insurance questions, answered.

Still unsure about your coverage? Call 781-255-0500 — we'll check before your visit.

Do I need a referral?

No. Most major plans don't require a referral — just walk in.

What will I pay?

For most plans you'll pay your standard office-visit copay — typically far less than an emergency-room visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

We offer affordable, posted self-pay rates. Check our pricing page or call us for a quote before you come in.

Do you accept Medicare?

Yes. Medicare is accepted, along with most major commercial plans.

Can you help with specialist or follow-up care?

Yes. We're affiliated with a range of specialists and can coordinate follow-up or hospital admission to the hospital of your choice.

How do I pay my bill?

You can pay online through Pay My Bill, or manage your account in the Patient Portal.
