When to come here vs. the ER

Most ER trips aren't ER-level emergencies.

If it's not immediately life-threatening—chest pain with no pulse, severe stroke symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding—we can almost certainly handle it here, faster and for a fraction of the cost.
