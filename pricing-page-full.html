Understanding your costs

What shapes your out-of-pocket cost.

Health insurance plans, benefits, deductibles, and coverage often change from year to year as insurers update policies and raise premiums. As a result, your out-of-pocket cost may differ from a previous visit — even for the same care.

Fee at the time of your visit

You’ll typically owe any copayment your plan requires. Afterward, we submit claims directly to your insurance carrier.

Your total fee responsibility

Your total, final fee depends on several factors, including your insurance, deductible, and the services rendered.

Your visit is typically made up of two parts

Part 1

Office visit

Every patient is billed for an Evaluation and Management (E/M) service based on the complexity of the evaluation, exam, medical decision-making, and care required—not simply the time spent.

Part 2

Additional services (when necessary)

Depending on your provider’s clinical judgment, added services may help diagnose or treat your condition. These may include:

  • Digital X-Rays
  • Laboratory testing
  • EKGs
  • Splinting or casting
  • Laceration (stitches) repair
  • IV fluids or medications
  • Other in-office procedures

Like most physician offices and hospital outpatient departments, each service is submitted as a separate insurance claim line. Together, these make up the total cost of your visit.

Example

A patient who comes in with a painful wrist after a fall may receive:

  • Part 1Office visit (Evaluation & Management)
  • Part 2Wrist X-Ray
  • Part 2Wrist splint

The office visit is your Part 1 charge; the X-Ray and splint are Part 2 services. They appear as separate lines on the insurance claim — but together, they represent a single urgent care visit.

Our commitment to price transparency

We understand that medical billing can be confusing. Our goal is to provide high-quality, affordable care while helping you understand the services you receive and any associated costs. If you have questions about your insurance benefits or expected charges, our team is happy to help explain them before or after your visit whenever possible.

How billing works

Three things to know before your visit.

No surprises, no card on file. Here is exactly how you'll be charged.

With insurance

You pay only your required copay at the time of your visit. Everything else is billed to your insurer first.

Without insurance

You pay only the initial visit fee today. Any added services are billed to you afterward, due on receipt.

You're always asked first

If we recommend a test, you'll see the price and agree before anything is done. No card is held at check-in.
