Who needs a DOT physical?

  • You hold or are applying for a commercial driver's license (CDL)
  • You drive a vehicle rated over 10,001 lbs
  • You carry 9 or more passengers for pay (15+ otherwise)
  • You transport placarded hazardous materials
  • You drive in interstate commerce

Bring paperwork for any ongoing condition—missing records for things like sleep apnea or heart disease can delay your certification.

What's checked

  • Vision—at least 20/40 in each eye, with peripheral and color checks
  • Hearing—ability to perceive a forced whisper at five feet
  • Blood pressure and pulse
  • Urinalysis—screens for underlying conditions (not a drug test)
  • A head-to-toe physical of your major systems

What to bring

  • A photo ID
  • A list of your current medications and prescribing providers
  • Your glasses or contacts, and hearing aids if you use them
  • Notes from your specialists for any condition (heart, diabetes, sleep apnea)
  • CPAP compliance data if you're treated for sleep apnea
  • The completed driver section of your exam form

Your medical certificate

  • Pass and you receive your Medical Examiner's Certificate—your “med card”
  • Valid for up to 24 months
  • May be issued for a shorter term if a condition needs monitoring
  • You leave with the certificate in hand the same visit

Good to know

The urinalysis in a DOT exam screens for health conditions—it is not a drug test. If your employer also requires a DOT drug screen, we can do that in the same visit.

Come rested, go easy on caffeine and salt beforehand, and bring your glasses or hearing aids—it gives you the best shot at certification on the first try.
