Why get vaccinated with us

A vaccine is one of the simplest ways to stay ahead of illness—a Tdap after a cut or for a new job, a hepatitis B series, or a TB skin test for school or work. There's no need to book ahead or sit in a waiting room for an appointment: walk in, get your shot, and you're done in minutes.

Common reasons to come in

  • A Tdap booster after a cut, burn, or puncture wound
  • Tdap or other shots required for a new job
  • A hepatitis B series for work, school, or travel
  • A TB skin test (PPD) for employment or enrollment
  • Catching up on a missed or overdue vaccine

Having a severe allergic reaction—trouble breathing, swelling, or hives after a shot? Call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
