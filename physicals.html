Why get your physical with us

A physical exam confirms you're healthy enough for what's ahead—a sport, a new job, a commercial license, or an elective surgery. Most just need a provider to check you over and sign a form, and that shouldn't take a week to schedule. Walk in any day we're open, see a provider, and leave with your form completed—no appointment, one flat price.

Common reasons to come in

  • A school or youth-sports physical before the season
  • A DOT exam for a commercial driver's license
  • A pre-employment exam required by a new employer
  • A 7D exam for school-transport drivers
  • Pre-op clearance before an elective procedure
  • A routine adult check-up or a required form signed

A physical is a routine, scheduled-type visit. If you're acutely ill or injured, let the front desk know so we can treat that first.
