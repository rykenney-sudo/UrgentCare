Urine drug testing

All of our drug testing is done by urine collection, performed on-site at our office. It's the most common method used in workplace programs and detects casual use over the past few days and chronic use for up to about a month.

  • In-house screen — same-day positive/negative result during the visit
  • Laboratory screen — sent to Quest Diagnostics for quantitative results with physician review in about 3 business days
  • Chain-of-custody collection — available when results need to hold up to scrutiny

How it works

  • Your company provides the test authorization and panel
  • We verify a photo ID and collect the sample under secure chain-of-custody
  • The sample is analyzed by the laboratory
  • A Medical Review Officer (MRO) reviews any non-negative result and confirms valid prescriptions
  • The verified result is reported back to your company

Good to know

A flagged result is often just a valid prescription. The Medical Review Officer contacts the donor to confirm that before any final result reaches the employer.

Marijuana and THC rules vary by state and by role, and DOT-regulated positions follow federal standards. We test to your written policy and requirements—we don't set them. Need an expanded panel or hair test? We arrange those through our reference lab.
