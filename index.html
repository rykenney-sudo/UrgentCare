Filling the gap

We’re Here to Care for Patients Impacted by the Closure of Norwood Hospital.

When Norwood Hospital closed, the community lost a trusted source of emergency-level care. We built our practice to fill that gap: experienced ER-trained clinicians, on-site imaging and labs, and the capacity to manage higher-acuity cases that other urgent cares send straight to the ER. Less than 1% of our patients are transferred — meaning most issues that would have sent you to Norwood Hospital are handled here, today, without the wait or the bill.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about visiting us, wait times, and worker's comp. Still have a question? Call us at 781-255-0500.

View all FAQs →
What is urgent care?

Urgent care is a medical practice providing prompt treatment of all non-life-threatening acute injuries and illnesses for patients of all ages on a walk-in basis.

When should I go to Norwood Urgent Care?

For any non-life-threatening illness or injury that needs same-day attention — cuts, sprains, fractures, infections, fevers, and more. For chest pain, stroke symptoms, or major trauma, call 911.

How long will I have to wait?

Most patients are seen within 5–10 minutes. Our current estimated wait is posted live at the top of this page.

What forms of insurance do you accept?

We accept most major plans, including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, Harvard Pilgrim, Tufts, United, Humana, MassHealth, WellSense, and Medicare. See our full insurance list for details.

What if I am currently not covered under health insurance?

Uninsured patients pay a flat, posted self-pay rate. You'll know exactly what your visit costs before you're seen — see our pricing transparency page.

Do you have a Worker's Comp program?

Yes. We handle workplace injuries, DOT and workers' physicals, and drug testing through our Employer Services. Call us to set up an account for your business.
