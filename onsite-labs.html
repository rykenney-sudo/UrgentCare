Why you might need a lab test

A quick lab test takes the guesswork out of a diagnosis. A throat swab tells strep from a virus, a urinalysis points to an infection, a rapid flu or mono test confirms what's going around, and a urine pregnancy or drug screen gives a clear answer in minutes. Because our samples are processed on-site, you get results during the same visit and leave with a treatment plan, not a "wait and see."

Common reasons to get tested

  • A sore throat or fever (strep test)
  • Fever, aches, and chills (flu A / B)
  • Fatigue and swollen glands (mono)
  • Burning or frequent urination (urinalysis)
  • A possible pregnancy (urine pregnancy)
  • A required drug screen or pre-treatment screening

Having a medical emergency—like chest pain, trouble breathing, or fainting? Call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
