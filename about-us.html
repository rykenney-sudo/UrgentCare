Our story

Where it started.

Norwood Urgent Care was founded with a simple mission: to provide convenient, high-quality healthcare when patients need it most. At a time when emergency rooms were crowded and primary care appointments could take days or weeks, our founders envisioned a better option—expert medical care delivered quickly, compassionately, and close to home.

As one of the first urgent care centers in the region, we helped redefine how patients access healthcare by providing hospital-quality treatment for everyday illnesses and injuries without the long wait. From the beginning, our focus has been on delivering exceptional care in a welcoming environment while respecting our patients’ time.

Building on the success of our original location, we expanded our services by opening Natick Urgent Care in 2017 and Bellingham Urgent Care in 2019, allowing us to serve even more families and employers throughout the MetroWest region. Today, our three independently owned centers offer on-site digital X-Ray, rapid laboratory testing, EKGs, occupational medicine services, and experienced medical providers dedicated to delivering efficient, evidence-based care.

While we’ve grown over the years, our values have remained the same. We are proud to be clinician-owned and independently operated, allowing every decision to be guided by what’s best for our patients—not corporate shareholders.

Today, Norwood Urgent Care, Natick Urgent Care, and Bellingham Urgent Care collectively care for approximately 125,000 patient visits each year. As we approach two decades of serving our communities, we remain committed to providing accessible, compassionate, and exceptional healthcare—every day, without an appointment.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything patients most often ask us — about visiting, wait times, insurance, and billing. Still have a question? Call us at 781-255-0500.

What is urgent care?

Urgent care is a medical practice providing prompt treatment of all non-life-threatening acute injuries and illnesses for patients of all ages on a walk-in basis.

When should I go to Norwood Urgent Care?

For any non-life-threatening illness or injury that needs same-day attention — cuts, sprains, fractures, infections, fevers, and more. For chest pain, stroke symptoms, or major trauma, call 911.

How long will I have to wait?

Most patients are seen within 10–20 minutes. Our current estimated wait is posted live at the top of the homepage.

What forms of insurance do you accept?

We accept most major plans, including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, Harvard Pilgrim, Tufts, United, Humana, MassHealth, WellSense, and Medicare. See our full insurance list for details.

What if I am currently not covered under health insurance?

Uninsured patients pay a flat, posted self-pay rate. You'll know exactly what your visit costs before you're seen — see our pricing transparency page.

Do you have a Worker's Comp program?

Yes. We handle workplace injuries, DOT and workers' physicals, and drug testing through our Employer Services. Call us to set up an account for your business.

What will I pay?

For most plans you'll pay your standard office-visit copay — typically far less than an emergency-room visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

We offer affordable, posted self-pay rates. Check our pricing page or call us for a quote before you come in.

Do you accept Medicare?

Yes. Medicare is accepted, along with most major commercial plans.

Can you help with specialist or follow-up care?

Yes. We're affiliated with a range of specialists and can coordinate follow-up or hospital admission to the hospital of your choice.

How do I pay my bill?

You can pay online through Pay My Bill, or manage your account in the Patient Portal.
